# Review the finished rehearsal

Ask another engineer to review your handover without watching you run the lab.

1. Can they find the exact migration settings and table list?
2. Can they distinguish measured 35 GiB evidence from a small development run?
3. Can they explain how old token/session continuity was tested?
4. Can they identify the first target write and rollback boundary?
5. Can they list the differences between this demo and the customer's two-account/EKS architecture?
6. Can they rebuild or reset without retrieving credentials from a published page?

**Deliverable:** a reviewed runbook and an explicit list of production-specific work remaining.
